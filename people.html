PEOPLE

The FOODPOWER team

FOODPOWER brings together archaeological, bioarchaeological, and biomolecular approaches to investigate changing foodways, inequality, and power in the ancient Andes.

PROJECT LEAD

Luis Flores-Blanco

Marie Curie Postdoctoral Fellow

BioArCh, Department of Archaeology, University of York

Luis Flores-Blanco is the researcher leading FOODPOWER. His research explores foodways, social inequality, political organization, and long-term social change in the ancient Andes, combining archaeological and biomolecular approaches.

ORCID: 0000-0003-2344-6542

SCIENTIST IN CHARGE

Oliver Craig

Director of BioArCh

BioArCh, Department of Archaeology, University of York

Oliver Craig is the FOODPOWER project supervisor. His research focuses on biomolecular archaeology, human diets, foodways, and the application of stable isotope and organic residue analysis to archaeological questions.

COLLABORATION

FOODPOWER is an interdisciplinary project.

The project brings together archaeological evidence, laboratory science, and biomolecular approaches to investigate how food became connected to social differentiation and political power.
